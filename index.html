📋 Step 1 — Patient Assessment

🌿 Step 2 — Non-Pharmacologic Measures

Always start here.

Dietary Fiber

  • Maximize tolerated dietary fiber within K⁺/PO₄ restrictions (goal 20–38 g/day).
  • Low-K⁺ sources: apples, berries, cabbage, cauliflower, green beans, carrots, barley.
  • Ground flaxseed 1–2 tbsp/day.

Fluid Optimization

  • Optimize within prescribed restriction.

Physical Activity

  • Aim 30 min/day as tolerated.

Bowel Habits

  • Respond to urge; regular time every 1–2 days.

Medication Review

Minimize: phosphate binders, oral iron, potassium binders, diuretics, opioids, antihistamines, psychotropics, CCBs.

💊 Step 3 — First-Line Laxatives

Safe in ESRD at unchanged doses: bisacodyl, docusate, senna, lactulose, PEG.

*PEG caution: avoid without physician supervision. Lactulose is most commonly used in advanced CKD.
🚫 Do NOT use in renal impairment:
  • Magnesium-containing laxatives (hypermagnesemia)
  • Phosphate-containing laxatives (acute phosphate nephropathy)
  • Bulk-forming agents (fluid restriction; polycarbophil adds calcium)

🔬 Step 4 — Second-Line & Refractory

GC-C Agonists

  • Linaclotide 72–145 μg daily
  • Plecanatide 3 mg daily

Chloride Channel Activator

  • Lubiprostone 24 μg BID

5-HT₄ Agonist

  • Prucalopride — reduce to 1 mg daily in CKD

Dual-Benefit (Hyperphosphatemia + Constipation)

Tenapanor — NHE3 inhibitor. FDA: 30 mg BID before meals; Japan: start 5 mg BID, titrate to 10–30 mg BID. Monitor diarrhea.

Rectal Therapies (PRN)

  • Glycerin or bisacodyl suppository PRN
  • Microlax enema — do NOT use Fleet enema
No BM ≥7 days: rule out fecal impaction / bowel obstruction.

👥 Step 5 — Special Populations

Critical: constipation → catheter migration / drainage failure.
  • Senna & bisacodyl may be first-line.
  • Consider synbiotics.
  • Tenapanor for phosphate + constipation.
  • Diarrhea (mycophenolate) more common.
  • If constipated: docusate 200 mg BID → sennosides 24 mg BID or PEG 17 g daily.
  • Fentanyl preferred opioid (less constipation).
  • Consider methylnaltrexone if refractory OIC.
  • Constipation worsens hyperkalemia.
  • Potassium binders may cause constipation.

Stepwise Pathway (Text)

  1. Red Flag Screening — any alarm → urgent evaluation.
  2. Non-Pharmacologic — fiber, fluid, activity, bowel habits, med review.
  3. First-Line — lactulose ± docusate/senna/bisacodyl (avoid Mg²⁺ & PO₄).
  4. Second-Line — linaclotide, plecanatide, lubiprostone, prucalopride (1 mg in CKD).
  5. Dual-Benefit — add tenapanor if hyperphosphatemia present.
  6. Refractory — rectal therapies PRN + specialist referral.
